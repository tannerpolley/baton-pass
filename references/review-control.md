# Review recovery

This reference owns recovery mechanics for solo work and selected workflows. Use the existing task, brief, review response, or handoff; no new tracking system is required.

## Acceptance and findings

Give workers and reviewers the same authorized outcomes/phases, non-goals, acceptance evidence, and material design boundaries. The solo agent or manager owns unresolved architecture, interfaces, authority, and security decisions. Routine reversible choices remain autonomous; selected planning gates still apply.

| Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Defect in a required outcome or real safety/integrity boundary | Replace the defective implementation under the root no-overengineering rule within authority, or report a blocker, even if old tests pass. |
| Unsupported claim | Correct the claim and disclose any required functionality still missing. |
| Wrong or duplicated responsibility | Remove the wrong or duplicated implementation; use the existing correct owner or simplest correct replacement while preserving required behavior and safeguards. |
| Optional hardening or future capability | Mark nonblocking; no automatic implementation, issue, experiment, or follow-up agent. |
| Material scope, security, or complexity change | Present evidence and smallest alternatives through native input; wait before affected actions. |

Tie blockers to an existing requirement or credible safety/integrity risk and concrete evidence. Investigate uncertain findings proportionately; a reviewer's preferred solution is not an instruction. If a necessary design assumption fails, expose the changed assumption rather than silently moving acceptance or claiming success under obsolete criteria.

Independent checks may use a separate oracle, method, fixture, or security boundary; copying the producer's protocol does not establish independence. An experiment answers its authorized question and stops; its result does not authorize another experiment or durable implementation.

## Stop and recover

A correction round is a review-driven revision followed by re-evaluation. The initial implementation/review is not a correction round; tool calls and individual findings are not rounds. Preserve the history for the same acceptance boundary across workers, reviewers, tasks, and renamed phases in existing handoff notes.

Pause before a third automatic correction round, earlier when a failure pattern recurs after a fix, or before support duplicates an existing responsibility or dominates the requested capability without demonstrated need. Size and test counts are warning signals, not acceptance criteria or a ban on necessary support.

1. Pause affected edits and corrective dispatch. Preserve the candidate, failing evidence, unrelated work, and safeguards. A delegated holder returns the evidence through its authorized parent/manager route.
2. Identify the recurring failure and responsible design boundary. Apply the root no-overengineering rule: remove its broken implementation and select an existing correct owner or the simplest correct replacement. Further bandaid layers over the defective path are not a recovery option.
3. Record the smallest justified recovery and its falsifying check in the existing task/handoff. A materially different recovery within settled authority may proceed; changed requirements, security boundaries, or material complexity need native user input. Another patch or renamed round alone is not recovery.
4. If the same boundary reaches another stop without a resolved design, report the blocker and obtain direction instead of self-authorizing another loop.

A stop is not success and never waives required checks, isolation, write scope, source identity, credential safety, cancellation, or evidence integrity. If required native input is broken or unsupported, report observed facts and stop dependent work; independent authorized work may continue.

## Evidence and endpoint

Keep findings and dispositions with their existing owner. Preserve decisive failures, reproduction inputs, and raw outputs when the deliverable or required audit needs them; this policy grants no deletion authority over user evidence or archives.

Refresh checks and reviews invalidated by the changed candidate, retain still-valid evidence, and complete selected review at the actual final candidate. Acceptance and authorized delivery end the work; optional advice does not start another phase or review campaign.
